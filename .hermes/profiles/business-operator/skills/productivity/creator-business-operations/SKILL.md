# Creator Business Operations

## Overview

This umbrella covers the user's recurring creator/business operating system: discovering and validating offers, turning lived experience into content leverage, publishing to social platforms safely, and producing daily operator-style briefings that convert data sources into action.

Use this skill when the work is not just writing content or sending a report, but strengthening a repeatable growth loop: story → proof asset → audience signal → offer hypothesis → publishing system → daily operating feedback.

## When to Use

- Interviewing the user to discover, package, validate, or price a high-ticket offer.
- Creating a content-led path from lived experience, story, or transformation into audience trust and paid products.
- Turning Jared/kids coding tutoring into a business offer: curriculum, Codewars/SoloLearn/project-based lessons, parent-facing progress reports, pricing/packages, outreach, and student motivation loops.
- Handling income-oriented career-growth work that the user routes into the business channel: GM Financial growth, cloud certs, resume/interview/salary planning, and SMART goals.
- Configuring or troubleshooting TikTok, Instagram/Meta, YouTube, X/Twitter, GIF/media, or brokered social publishing flows.
- Producing or maintaining the user's morning report, trend radar, Google Workspace context, email cleanup policy, or portfolio heartbeat.
- Building scheduled creator operations that combine source acquisition, generated media, metadata, upload logs, cleanup, and human review gates.

## Offer Architecture

Default posture: interview, do not lecture.

- Ask 1–3 questions at a time.
- Keep the interview laconic and paced; reflect only the strongest signal.
- Do not flatten a complex personal story into a product before the transformation arc lands.
- Separate teachable knowledge, coaching insight, technical execution, accountability, network/context, and story-based authority.
- Validate high-ticket pricing through measurable transformation, ROI, saved time, avoided risk, identity/status upgrade, urgency, and willingness to pay.

Six-stage flow:

1. Background discovery.
2. Skill extraction.
3. Market alignment.
4. Offer architecture.
5. High-ticket validation.
6. Positioning and launch path.

When the user is not ready to sell, build a content proof engine first: public transformation log → audience questions → lead magnet/proof asset → low-ticket starter → cohort/community → high-ticket offer.

## Daily Operator Reports

The morning report is a compact operating brief, not a generic greeting.

Required ingredients unless the user changes them:

- Chicago local pulse.
- Compact bullish/bearish market read plus one thing to watch.
- Portfolio heartbeat for Robinhood accounts when tools are available.
- Search + social pulse in one-line bullets.
- Game-changing AI/coding news in minimal Discord-readable bullets.
- Capability/trend radar with reusable-asset framing.
- Google Workspace work source / operating pulse when connected.
- Email priority + cleanup mini-section following approved cleanup rules.
- "You'll probably find this interesting" aligned with AI agents, coding leverage, markets, internet culture, automation, community/client websites, collectibles, or asymmetric opportunities.
- Numerology as reflective/for-fun language, not deterministic claims.
- One daily challenge and one big priority prompt.

Tone: direct, warm, concise, operator-aesthetic, no dense tables, no fake current facts. Use tools for weather, current news, market conditions, and dates.

## Social Platform Publishing

Publishing integrations must be built around exact platform modes, scopes, and review requirements.

1. Identify the publishing mode: read, draft upload, direct publish, metadata edit, deletion, or analytics.
2. Prefer low-friction pilots: TikTok draft/inbox (`video.upload`) before direct posting; private/draft/self-only first for automated YouTube/social workflows unless public posting is explicitly approved.
3. Keep secrets out of git; use chmod `600` env files or external secret paths.
4. Build dry-run helpers before real API calls; print endpoint/mode/scope/payload shape without secrets.
5. Persist OAuth/PKCE verifier state across auth URL and code exchange processes.
6. Log platform ids/URLs before cleanup and restrict cleanup to allowlisted cache/output directories.
7. For app review, provide concrete copy/paste field packets, scope justifications, user-control language, legal-page drafts, icons/demo assets as needed.

## Email and Source Triage

Treat Gmail/Workspace data as read-only until the user approves destructive actions.

- Prioritize billing/receipts, Chase/Apple/Card, cloud/AI API services, Robinhood, tickets/venues, myQ/Chamberlain, GitGuardian/security, official/vehicle notices, and rent-impacting logistics.
- Landing is priority only for standby/rent-impacting logistics, active stay changes, payments/rent, parking, mail/packages, pets, or support.
- Do not summarize Credit Karma unless credit score drop, serious credit risk, identity, or duplicate-charge signal appears.
- Summarize useful source emails (TLDR from the right account, Kino Body, Daily Stoic, Grammarly Insights) before cleanup decisions.
- Known junk/spam cleanup may recur after approval; ambiguous or important Workspace actions still require confirmation.

## Managed Agentic AI Services Operations

For small-business Hermes/agentic-AI service ventures, sell measurable outcomes and managed operations rather than a bare software installation. Keep this venture separate from unrelated businesses even when they share one Google account. Build a real private Google Drive operating library under the explicitly selected account: native offer/capability/sales/audit/onboarding/security/delivery Docs, an ROI and pipeline Sheet, a client-delivery area, and a visually inspected flier.

Default initial offer architecture can use a paid AI Opportunity Audit followed by setup fee + monthly managed service. Define workflow, integration, user, support, variable-cost, human-approval, and change-order boundaries; never promise unlimited AI or unrestricted autonomous action. Keep permanent business name/contact details as explicit placeholders until selected rather than inventing them.

## Personalized Coding Curriculum Operations

For kids/teens coding tutoring, default to a premium **1:1** offer; optional **2:1** delivery remains instructor-controlled. Build a teacher-first learning track alongside student materials, and center the program on algorithm patterns, debugging, documentation literacy, responsible AI use, and demonstrable projects.

When producing the offer in Google Workspace, create real private Drive artifacts—not merely a plan: native Docs, a progress Sheet, separate teacher/customer areas, and a visually inspected flier. Verify representative artifacts and ownership through API read-back, and request separate approval before changing sharing permissions.

## Common Pitfalls

1. Over-questioning the user during story collection.
2. Positioning trauma or lived experience as a product before confirming audience and consent.
3. Treating social read scopes as upload permissions.
4. Claiming upload readiness before dry-run/credential/scope verification.
5. Inventing live trend, market, weather, or workspace facts instead of using tools.
6. Making recurring reports too wordy for Discord/mobile reading.
7. Deleting or unsubscribing from ambiguous email without explicit scope approval.
