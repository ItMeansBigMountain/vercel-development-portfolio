# Minimal table-first morning report news format

Session signal: user said the morning report news was too wordy/long and requested a more minimalistic table presentation.

## Durable preference

For the news/trend sections of the morning operator report, prefer compact Markdown tables over paragraph summaries.

## Recommended tables

- Market pulse: `Bias | Case | Why | Watch`
- Search + social pulse: `Signal | Where | Why it matters | Action/Ignore`
- AI + coding news: `News | Impact | Leverage move`
- Trend radar: `Trend | Velocity | Spread | Money | Saturation | Capability | Kill/Scale`

## Style constraints

- Keep each cell short: phrase fragments are better than full paragraphs.
- Use bullets only for quick opener, location pulse, work pulse, daily challenge, and hard truth.
- Avoid lengthy explanations unless the user asks for expansion.
- If a current-data source cannot be verified, note it briefly in the relevant table cell instead of adding a paragraph.
