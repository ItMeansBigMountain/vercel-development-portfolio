# Faceless YouTube younger-audience script style — 2026-06

Use this when scripting the user's faceless/newsletter YouTube videos.

## User correction

The user said the scripts were sharing critical data, but sounded too expert/professional, too technical, and hard to follow for a younger audience.

## Style rule

Keep the facts, but translate them into a punchy, easy-to-follow story that feels like a smart friend warning you about something that matters.

## Required shape

- Hook first, data second.
- One main idea at a time.
- Short sentences.
- Plain words over expert language.
- Relatable stakes: money, status, attention, discipline, health, dating, school/work, social media, identity.
- Use light slang only when it feels natural; do not force Gen-Z parody.
- Explain technical claims with a concrete image or daily-life example.
- Avoid analyst phrases like "this indicates," "meaningful shift," "consumer behavior," "dopamine regulation," "my read," and "the implication is."
- Avoid rigid sections: no spoken "intro," "body," "conclusion," "data point one," or "let's unpack."

## Rewrite examples

Too expert:
> This indicates a meaningful shift in consumer behavior around digital dependence and dopamine regulation.

Better:
> Your phone is not just distracting you. It is training your brain to need chaos.

Too technical:
> The study suggests a correlation between sleep deprivation and impaired executive function.

Better:
> When you sleep like trash, your brain stops acting like the boss and starts acting like a tired intern.

Too professional:
> There are several important factors to consider before adopting this habit.

Better:
> Before you copy this habit, you need to know the part nobody puts in the caption.

## QA checklist before render/upload

- Would a 16–28 year old understand the point without pausing?
- Does the first sentence create curiosity or tension?
- Did we keep the source facts but remove academic/consultant wording?
- Are captions short enough to read instantly?
- Is there one clear emotional payoff by the end?
