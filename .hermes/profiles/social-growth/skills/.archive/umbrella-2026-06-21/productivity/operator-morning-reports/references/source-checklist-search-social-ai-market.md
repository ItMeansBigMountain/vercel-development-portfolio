# Morning report source checklist for search/social/AI/market pulses

Use this when generating or maintaining the daily operator morning report so the trend sections stay current, verifiable, and concise.

## Search pulse

Prefer source-backed current trend lists:
- Google Trends Daily Search Trends / Realtime Trends when accessible.
- Credible trend recap/news sources only as fallback.
- Include both single words and phrases when notable.
- For each item, explain what changed and why it matters in one line.

## Social pulse

Look for cross-platform convergence, not just one noisy viral post:
- X/Twitter, Reddit, TikTok/YouTube culture recaps, Hacker News/Product Hunt for tech, and major news/social dashboards when available.
- Distinguish `velocity` from `durability`: fast spike, sustained discussion, or early niche signal.
- If a topic cannot be verified with current tools, say so rather than inventing.

## AI + coding news

Prioritize game-changing items:
- Major model releases or capability jumps.
- Agent/coding workflow shifts.
- Developer platform changes that alter cost, speed, distribution, or automation leverage.
- Practical move for the user: what to test, save, ignore, or build around.

## Market pulse

Do not guess bullish/bearish state. Use current market commentary and price context. Present:
- Bull case.
- Bear case.
- What to watch today.

## Format reminder

Keep everything Discord-readable: compact bullets, no Markdown tables, no long paragraphs, and one actionable move per signal.
