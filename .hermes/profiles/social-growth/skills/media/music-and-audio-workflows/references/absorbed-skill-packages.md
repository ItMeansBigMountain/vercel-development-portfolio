# Absorbed Skill Packages

Original packages were archived intact after their class-level guidance was folded into `music-and-audio-workflows`.

- `songwriting-and-ai-music`: lyric craft, structure, meter/rhyme, Suno prompt engineering, and phonetic tricks.
- `heartmula`: HeartMuLa installation, CUDA/GPU notes, lyrics/tags usage, and generation pitfalls.
- `songsee`: audio spectrogram and feature visualization CLI guidance.
- `audiocraft-audio-generation`: MusicGen/AudioGen/EnCodec workflows, troubleshooting, and advanced usage.
