# 14-Day Self-Improvement Challenge — Video Notes

Session-specific source notes from the user's transcript chunks, to guide the morning stand-up challenge section.

## Core setup

- The challenge is framed as immediate action, not passive consumption: do not wait until motivation is high.
- Original physical tracker: days 1–14 down the side, habits across the top, visible on the wall.
- User prefers Hermes to ask and track it instead of relying only on paper.

## Core mindset

- The challenge is designed for **worst days**, not best days.
- The one-second rule is the central mechanism: as soon as the user does one second of a habit, it counts.
- Examples:
  - Meditation: one breath counts; recommended Medito daily meditation, 3 minutes when possible.
  - Gratitude: one thought, written note, or direct message counts.
  - Exercise: one rep counts; push-ups to failure are the fallback for rest/bad days.
  - Learning: any self-improvement learning counts.
  - Socializing/flirting: any small social touchpoint counts.
- The emotional logic: tiny completion creates a winner effect and prevents a bad day from turning into a 3–5 day destructive spiral.

## Morning-report usage

Keep this section short. Use it as a tiny behavioral nudge, not a lecture.

Recommended phrasing:

- **14-Day Challenge — Day X/14**
- **Rule** — *One breath/rep/minute/social touch counts. This is for bad days, not perfect days.*
- **Boxes** — Meditation / Gratitude / Exercise / Learning / Social
- **Tiny floor** — {one very small action, e.g. “one breath + one gratitude text before noon”}
- Reply: `done: meditation gratitude exercise learning social`

## Pitfall

Do not over-repeat the transcript or make medical/mental-health claims in the report. The useful takeaway is behavioral: visible accountability, tiny minimums, consistency after bad days, and avoiding destructive spirals.
