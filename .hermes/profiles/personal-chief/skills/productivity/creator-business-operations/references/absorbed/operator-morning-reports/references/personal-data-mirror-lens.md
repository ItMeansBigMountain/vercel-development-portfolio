# Personal data mirror lens

Session signal: the user articulated a durable product/mission thesis around consumer data liberation and self-understanding.

## Core thesis

The user believes people should be able to read and understand their own behavioral data in a smart, traceable way. Companies already infer identity, attention, desire, habits, and intent from phone/app/location/screen/work data; the consumer should get that insight back as self-knowledge.

Useful framing:

- “They already track you. We help you understand yourself.”
- “A personal data mirror that shows you who you are becoming.”
- “Consumer data sovereignty + subconscious pattern recognition.”

## How this affects morning reports

When interpreting data sources like Gmail, Grammarly, calendar, Drive activity, content/newsletters, app usage, or writing output, do not only summarize events. Pull one or two traceable behavioral insights:

- What is the user focusing on repeatedly?
- What topics, people, or systems keep resurfacing?
- What tone is the user emitting in writing/work?
- What looks like life admin, avoidance, dopamine pull, discipline, or leverage?
- What identity is being reinforced by their digital behavior?
- What does the data suggest about their current “frequency” or impact on the world?

Keep the language grounded. Avoid mystical overreach unless clearly framed as reflective metaphor.

## Good report phrasing

- “Your data is pointing toward X pattern.”
- “The traceable signal: X showed up across email/calendar/work context.”
- “This is a self-knowledge datapoint, not a judgment.”
- “Your current digital exhaust suggests your attention is split between X and Y.”

## Product angle to preserve

Future app ideas should consider user-owned data dashboards that ingest personal exports/APIs and produce explainable, auditable insights about:

- communication tone and writing patterns
- work/project focus
- interests and content diet
- location/screen-time/attention patterns when available
- spending/subscription behavior
- relationships/social energy
- health/fitness/sleep signals

The product should emphasize traceability: show the source signals that led to an insight so the user can verify or reject the interpretation.
