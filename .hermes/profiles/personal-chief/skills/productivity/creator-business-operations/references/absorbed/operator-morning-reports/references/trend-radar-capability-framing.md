# Trend Radar + Capability Framing Update

## Session signal

The user explicitly pivoted from project-oriented planning to capability/system-oriented operating. They asked to include this in the morning report after discussing a Trend Radar capability for spotting viral internet trends and mapping them to shippable MVP experiments.

## Durable lesson

Morning reports should not become a list of apps/projects. They should help the user operate a capability-building system:

- Trend Intelligence: discover emerging internet/culture/tech/consumer trends.
- Rapid MVP Factory: turn trends into small experiments quickly.
- Distribution/Feedback Loops: define kill/scale signals and reuse what works.

Projects are temporary vehicles. The report may mention a project, but only as the current vehicle for building or validating a reusable capability.

## Required Trend Radar details

Include 3–5 trends when current-data tools allow it. For each trend, keep it brief and score qualitatively or numerically:

- Velocity: how quickly attention is moving.
- Cross-platform spread: whether signal exists beyond one surface.
- Monetization potential: whether a small product, service, tool, or content wedge can plausibly capture value.
- Saturation risk: whether the space is already crowded or copycat-heavy.
- Capability to strengthen: the durable reusable asset/workflow this trend suggests.
- Experiment to run: a small Vercel-first or no-code/low-code test when useful.
- Kill/scale signal: what would prove whether to stop or double down.

## Tone

Use language like: “capability to strengthen,” “reusable asset,” “experiment to run,” and “kill/scale signal.” Keep it direct, warm, and operator-like; avoid fluffy motivation or long essays.
