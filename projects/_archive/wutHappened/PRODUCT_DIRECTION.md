# WutHappened — Merge Source for stockNews

`wutHappened` and `stockNews` are the same project.

This folder should be treated as a merge/source archive. The active product direction now lives in:

```text
../stockNews/PRODUCT_DIRECTION.md
```

## Keep from this folder

- The “what happened today that matters to my portfolio?” framing.
- News aggregation experiments.
- Script/image/video generation ideas for optional daily recaps.
- Portfolio-aware relevance ideas.

## Active merged product

The unified app should let users import or enter their stock portfolio, then receive daily news, sentiment, risk, and catalyst explanations tied directly to their holdings.

Portfolio imports should support OAuth/vendor integrations where possible, plus CSV, JSON, manual lists, and vendor exports.
