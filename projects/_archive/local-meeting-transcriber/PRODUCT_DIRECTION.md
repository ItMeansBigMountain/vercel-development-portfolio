# Local Meeting Transcriber Direction

Goal: a free/private way to record, transcribe, and track meetings with insights over time.

## Core features

- Local audio import/recording workflow.
- Local transcription when possible; optional external providers later.
- Meeting summaries, action items, decisions, risks, and follow-ups.
- Searchable meeting memory over time.
- Insight trends: repeated blockers, people, commitments, deadlines.
