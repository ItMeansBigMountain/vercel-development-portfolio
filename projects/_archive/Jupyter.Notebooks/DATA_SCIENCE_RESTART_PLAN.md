# Data Science Restart Plan

This project should become the research lab for technical analysis bots, statistical analysis, trend plotting, and notebook-driven experimentation.

## Focus areas

- Market technical analysis notebooks.
- Statistical trend studies and hypothesis tests.
- Bot research notebooks before productionizing strategies.
- Clean reusable datasets and chart templates.
- Backtesting experiments with strong disclaimers and reproducibility.
