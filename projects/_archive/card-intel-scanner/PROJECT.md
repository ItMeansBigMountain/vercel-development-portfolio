## Notes

The project is now an unofficial Pokémon trading card scanner and price aggregation app. It uses browser OCR/live camera scanning for image-assisted identification, the public Pokémon TCG API for card metadata/prices, condition/grade lenses for valuation assumptions, a local browser watchlist, and eBay sold-comps links for market reality checks.
